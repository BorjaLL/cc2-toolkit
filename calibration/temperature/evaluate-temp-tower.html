How to read a temperature tower

Elegoo CC2 tower from build_tower.py, 9 blocks, hottest at the bottom (230 °C by default, --top) to 40 °C cooler at the top, 5 °C per block. About 10 minutes once the print is cold.

1Let it cool
Off the plate, 10 min. Warm PLA bends instead of snapping.
2Look
Strings, overhang, bridge, surface. Under a raking light (phone torch from the side).
3Break it
Bend each block between two pliers. This is the check that matters most.
4Score + pick
The guided reading below picks for you and writes a summary you can copy.

Judge the middle of each block. The bottom 1-2 mm of a block is still changing temperature from the block below.

2. The five checks, cold vs right vs hot

too cold (top of tower)what you wanttoo hot (bottom of tower)

C. Stringing

important for looks, fixable with retraction too

Look across the round hole and between the cone and the wall. Hold it against a dark background.

A. Overhang

important

Look at the underside of the curved lip from below and the side, with the torch raking across it.

B. Bridge

nice to have

Look up into the rectangle hole at its roof.

D. Surface + number

nice to have, matters more for matte PLA

Read the number, run a fingernail over the face, tilt it in the light.

E. Layer bond (snap test)

most important: a fail here rules the block out

Grip the tower with pliers just below a block, second pliers on the block, bend sideways. Or flex the overhang lip (A) with your thumb. Start at the top (coldest).

3. How the pick is made

Example shape: the cold top fails the snap test, the hot bottom strings. Pick inside the green band, upper middle.

  1. Floor = coldest block that passes the snap test. Never print at or below a block that snapped.
  2. Ceiling = hottest block with clean strings and overhang.
  3. Pick the middle of floor..ceiling, rounding up when the band has an even count: hotter gives bond margin and copes with faster prints.
  4. No ceiling found (even the hottest block looks fine)? Pick floor + 5.
  5. Band empty (strings appear before bond is good)? Pick the bond floor and fix strings with retraction.

Then: record it with your filament notes (see filaments/) and run the flow and retraction tests at that temperature.

Matte PLA notes

Matte PLA has weaker layer bond than plain PLA, so the snap test usually sets the floor higher (often 210-215). The matte look fades toward gloss as it gets hotter: if finish matters, pick the coldest block that still passes the snap test with margin, and note where the gloss starts.
